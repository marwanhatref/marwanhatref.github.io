Power BI · HR

HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Intelligence Dashboard

Developed an interactive HR analytics dashboard to track core workforce metrics and uncover primary drivers behind employee turnover across 1,464 employees. The dashboard isolates key attrition indicators across departmental structures, salary slabs, job roles, and employee experience levels to enable data-driven talent retention strategies. Key Metrics & Insights.

  • Identified primary attrition drivers, pinpointing a 16.05% turnover rate concentrated heavily in Administration (37%) and early-career roles.
  • Uncovered flight risks in salary brackets under 10 LPA and among employees with under 5 years of tenure.

Power BI · Marketing

Customer Segmentaion & Marketing Campaign Intelligence Dashboard

Developed an interactive customer analytics dashboard to evaluate spending behavior, purchasing channels, and demographic trends across 2.24K customers generating $1.35M in total revenue. The dashboard tracks key customer demographics, product category performance, and purchase distribution across store, web, and catalog channels to optimize marketing strategy and customer targeting.

  • Analyzed channel performance, revealing store purchases as the primary channel (38.97%), followed by web orders (27.22%) and catalog sales.
  • Identified core revenue drivers, highlighting Wines and Meat Products as the top-performing product categories.

How I work with SMBs

Small and medium businesses don't need a data science department — they need someone who can look at what's already tracked, in whatever tool it lives in, and turn it into a decision by Friday.

01

Start with the business question

Before opening a tool, I ask what decision this analysis needs to support — pricing, staffing, stock, retention.

02

Clean and connect the data

SQL and Python for the heavy lifting, Excel and Power BI for the model the team will actually keep using.

03

Hand over something usable

A dashboard, not a data dump — with the two or three numbers that matter surfaced first.
